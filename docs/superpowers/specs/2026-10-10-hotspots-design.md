# Where to look: hotspots, the Auto camera and "Happening now"

October 2026. The village has plenty going on, but a viewer who opens the page lands on a free camera over the valley and has to find a fight, a fire or a wedding through the chronicle. This stage of the viewer-engagement plan points them at it. The village is still watched, not steered: nothing here reaches the sim.

## What happens

- **Hotspots.** Once a second, with the status, the server's digest ranks what is worth watching right now (`server/crates/server/src/hotspots.rs`) and sends the best five in `Status.hotspots`, so a viewer who connects has them at once. Each names what it is (`HotspotKind`), whom to follow (a villager, one of the dead, a raider, the dragon or a ghost; none for a fire nobody is fighting yet), where it is, a short line of text and a score from 0 to 100. Roughly, from the top: the dragon (100), a boss (95), a burning home (80 to 90, fiercer is higher), a murderer striking (92), a fight to the death between villagers (88), a wedding, funeral or trial (85 while the rite is spoken, 70 while the village gathers, 50 at the feast), an orc raid (82), a brawl (72), a murderer stalking their next victim (64), a fight against the dead (58 and up, more with every villager in it), a quarrel (52), a ghost (48), the dead abroad with nobody fighting them (45), a romance (40), a tune with an audience (30 and up, more with the crowd), someone making up after a row (34), and on a quiet day one of each of: someone reading a billboard, someone riding out, a child at play, a friendly chat, someone at the anvil or training. One hotspot per subject; the line of text changes seldom (a tune says "to a crowd" rather than counting heads) so the status is not resent for nothing.
- **The Auto camera.** On by default and remembered in the browser (`fsw.auto`). When the viewer has not touched the camera for 30 seconds and follows nobody, it follows the top hotspot whose subject is on the page and in sight (a villager indoors is not), or glides to its place when it has no subject. It stays on a shot at least 20 seconds, cuts at once when that shot is over or something 20 points better starts, and after a minute moves on to anything within 15 points, so a quiet stretch still tours the village (`src/ui/director.ts`). Following through the Auto camera does not open a page. Panning, zooming, turning, clicking or choosing someone always wins; 🎬 Auto in the top bar (on a phone, the chip above the tabs) turns it on and off.
- **Happening now.** When a dragon, boss, fire, raid, ceremony, battle, brawl or stalking hotspot appears that was not there a moment ago, a card says so for twelve seconds with **Watch**, which follows its subject and opens their page. A kind is carded at most once every 90 seconds, so a fight that changes hands is one card; what was already going on when the page opened is not carded.

## What was left out

- **Tension-driven pacing**, a minimap and notifications for a favourite villager are later stages; they can reuse the scores.
- No new wire event: the hotspots ride in `Status`, which every viewer already gets at most once a second. Older clients ignore the field, so the protocol version is unchanged.
