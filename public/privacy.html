Privacy

Fantasy Sim World is a village that runs on one server around the clock. You watch it in your browser; you do not make an account, and nothing you do changes the world.

What this site keeps

A cookie holds your seat while you watch, so the server knows you are one of the viewers it has room for. Your browser keeps your sound settings, your chat name and your place in line in its own storage; they never leave it except for the chat name, which the other viewers see next to what you write. What you write in the chat is shown to everyone watching and is not kept once the server restarts.

The server sees your IP address, as every website does. It uses it to hand out seats and to slow down anyone trying to guess the admin's password, and may write it to its logs when that happens.

Ads

The site shows ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the server and the language model behind the villagers. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, to measure them and, if you agree, to choose ads by your interests. Viewers in Europe are asked first, and you can change your answer at any time under ⚙ Settings, "Privacy and ad choices".

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, or turn off ads chosen by your interests in Google's ad settings.
